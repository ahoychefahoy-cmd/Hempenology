01 / THE BEGINNING

Not just a name.
A way of looking at things.

Hempenology brings together a love of ingredients, creativity and the natural world.

We believe the best things begin with curiosity: knowing where things come from, respecting the process, and taking the time to make something properly. This is our independent home for that journey.
